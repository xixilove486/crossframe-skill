# Source deconfliction fact card

- Case: P03
- Issuer: Defense Intelligence Agency, Defense Counterintelligence and Human Intelligence Center, Office of Counterintelligence
- Source: CI Glossary -- Terms & Definitions of Interest for DoD CI Professionals
- Version marker: 2011-05-02 edition

- Cutoff check: the identified version predates 2026-08-02T00:00:00Z.

## Neutral facts

- The glossary defines deconfliction as sharing collection information across agencies to reduce duplicated effort, unintended repeated use of the same source, and circular reporting.
- The glossary attributes that definition to DoDI S-5240.17, CI Collection, dated 2009-01-12.
- The glossary provides terminology and does not assess any particular media outlet, policy, or success claim.
