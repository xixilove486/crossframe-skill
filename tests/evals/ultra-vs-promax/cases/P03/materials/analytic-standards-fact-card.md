# Analytic standards fact card

- Case: P03
- Issuer: Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI)
- Source: Intelligence Community Directive 203, Analytic Standards
- Version marker: technical amendment dated 2022-12-21 to the directive signed 2015-01-02
- Official document: https://www.dni.gov/files/documents/ICD/ICD-203.pdf
- Cutoff check: the identified version predates 2026-08-02T00:00:00Z.

## Neutral facts

- ICD 203 requires analytic products to describe the quality and credibility of underlying sources, data, and methodologies.
- Listed source-quality factors include accuracy, completeness, age, continued currency, access, validation, motivation, possible bias, and expertise.
- The directive separates the quantity of source material from its quality when explaining confidence and uncertainty.
- It also requires a distinction among underlying information, assumptions, and analytic judgments, plus consideration of plausible alternatives and contrary information.
- The directive supplies analytic standards; it does not assess any particular policy report or media corpus.
