## Neutral facts

- The memorandum covers federal-agency reliance on AI outputs that inform, influence, decide, or execute agency decisions or actions.
- It identifies decisions about access to, eligibility for, or revocation of critical government resources or services as presumed rights-impacting uses.
- For covered rights- or safety-impacting uses, it establishes practices that include impact assessment, real-world testing, independent evaluation, ongoing monitoring, periodic review, and risk mitigation.
- It also addresses trained human oversight, public notice, notice of adverse decisions, human consideration and remedy, and an accessible human alternative where the stated conditions make one practicable.
- The memorandum treats mission benefits and risks together; it does not evaluate any particular city, service, vendor, or model.
