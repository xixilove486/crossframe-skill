## Neutral facts

- AI RMF 1.0 is voluntary, rights-preserving, non-sector-specific, and use-case-agnostic.
- Its four risk-management functions are Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage.
- The framework treats trustworthy AI as context-dependent and calls for risks to individuals, organizations, and society to be identified, measured, prioritized, and managed across the lifecycle.
- Its trustworthiness characteristics include validity and reliability, safety, security and resilience, accountability and transparency, explainability and interpretability, privacy enhancement, and fairness with harmful bias managed.
- The framework does not determine the outcome of any particular public-service deployment.
