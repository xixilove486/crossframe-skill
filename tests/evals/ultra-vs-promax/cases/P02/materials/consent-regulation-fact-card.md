## Neutral facts

- Article 4(11) defines consent using the criteria freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous, expressed by a statement or clear affirmative action.
- Recital 32 distinguishes affirmative action from silence, pre-selected choices, or inactivity.
- Article 7 places the burden of demonstrating consent on the controller and requires withdrawal to be as easy as giving consent.
- Recitals 42 and 43 discuss genuine choice, detriment, and imbalance when assessing whether consent is freely given.
- These provisions are jurisdiction-specific legal criteria and do not decide the facts of any particular platform implementation.
