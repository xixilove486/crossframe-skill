# Consent guidance fact card

- Case: P02
- Issuer: European Data Protection Board (EDPB)
- Source: Guidelines 05/2020 on consent under Regulation 2016/679
- Version marker: version 1.1, adopted 2020-05-04
- Official record: https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/guideline/guidelines-052020-on-consent-under-regulation-2016679_en
- Cutoff check: the identified version predates 2026-08-02T00:00:00Z.

## Neutral facts

- The guidelines analyze consent through the elements of a freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous indication of wishes.
- They treat genuine choice and control as relevant to whether consent is freely given.
- They discuss detriment, imbalance, conditionality, and granularity as factors that can affect the validity of consent.
- They state that withdrawing consent should be as easy as giving it and that controllers must be able to demonstrate valid consent.
- The guidelines do not determine the status or evolution of any particular platform design.
